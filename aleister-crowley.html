Aleister Crowley: myth, controversy, and modern reflection

Crowley is a polarising figure. Read carefully: his life and work raise questions about will, responsibility, and how ritual can be used — for harm or healing.

Context and caution

Many readers are drawn to the dramatic stories around Crowley. A clearer takeaway is to study the ethics of will and ritual: how practices designed to change the self can also amplify existing wounds if not grounded in care.

This is not an endorsement, but an invitation to examine historical ideas with contemporary ethics: what can ritual teach about discipline and what must be rejected?
