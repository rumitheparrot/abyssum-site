Manly P. Hall: esoteric wisdom and modern meaning

Hall popularised deep symbolic traditions and argued that ritual and story provide frameworks for moral growth. His work can be read as a set of lenses for understanding personal change.

Why symbolism matters

Symbolic language connects inner experience to larger patterns. Hall's essays suggest that myth and ritual can act like tools — not to escape reality, but to name and reframe it.

For people grappling with identity, loss or transformation, these frameworks offer metaphors that make inner change legible and actionable.
